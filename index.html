Questions? We've got answers.

What AI models does NexaAI use?

NexaAI leverages a multi-model architecture including GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini Pro. We automatically route your requests to the best model for the task, or you can manually select your preference.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. We use enterprise-grade encryption and never use your data to train public models. For enterprise customers, we offer on-premise deployment and isolated VPC options.

Can I use NexaAI for my whole team?

Absolutely. NexaAI is built for collaboration. You can create shared workspaces, invite team members, and manage permissions with ease.

What happens after my free trial ends?

You'll be moved to our Starter plan automatically. All your data and projects will remain intact, but you'll have the daily limits of the free tier.

Do you offer a student or nonprofit discount?

Yes, we offer 50% off for verified students and non-profit organizations. Please reach out to our support team with your credentials.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.
